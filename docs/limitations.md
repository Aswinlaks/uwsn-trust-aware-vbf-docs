# Limitations

| Limitation | What it means |
|---|---|
| **One scenario** | One topology family, density, traffic pattern and simulator. Collisions are the only loss mechanism and there is no ambient noise, so results may not transfer to noisy or fading channels. |
| **Evidence status** | Only Study A is confirmatory in the strict sense. Studies B and C are prespecified replications and comparisons within the same scenario; development results are exploratory. The plans were frozen by hash but not lodged with an external registry. |
| **Recovery amendment** | Study C was interrupted by accident and completed under a documented recovery amendment, not in uninterrupted compliance with its plan. |
| **Comparator** | The channel-aware comparator is an adapted version, not the published model. Its maximum rules have no usable operating point at the frozen thresholds, and an over-dispersion statistic is missing for Study C. |
| **Intervals and seeds** | With few network clusters, the nominal coverage of the intervals is not established. The seeds were absent from all local records before use, but global non-use cannot be proven. |
| **Threat model** | Lying observers follow two simulated policies; collusion and position falsification were not studied. |
| **Coverage** | Between 7 % and 32 % of active droppers had fewer than three judging observers and could not be scored by median fusion. |
| **Literature** | The related-work review was targeted, not a systematic database search. Values reported by other studies are not comparable with ours. |
